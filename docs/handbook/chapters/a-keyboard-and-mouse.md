# A. Keyboard and Mouse

The keys and mouse actions of the desktop, in one place. The keys of each program are in its chapter; the table at the end points to them.

## The desktop

| Keys | What they do | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| ++super+l++ | Locks the screen. ++super++ is the key with the Windows logo. | 26 |
| ++ctrl+alt+v++ | Shows the clipboard history. | 36 |
| ++ctrl+c++ / ++ctrl+x++ / ++ctrl+v++ | Copy / cut / paste, in the amadeOS programs and most others. | 21 |
| ++ctrl+q++ | Quits the program, in most amadeOS programs. | 21 |
| ++f11++ | Full screen and back, in the games, Fractals and Firefox. | 23 |
| ++alt++ and the underlined letter | Opens a menu of the menu bar, or presses a button with an underlined letter. | — |
| ++esc++ | Closes a menu, and most dialogs, without choosing. | — |
| ++ctrl+alt+f2++ | Switches to a text console, for repairs. | 57 |
| ++ctrl+alt+f7++ | Switches back to the graphical screen. | 57 |

## The mouse

| Where | Left button | Middle button | Right button |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The backdrop** | Closes an open menu | — | The Workspace Menu (24) |
| **A title bar** | Click: makes the window active; drag: moves it | — | — |
| **The window menu button** | Click: the window menu; double click: closes the window (23) | — | — |
| **The edges and corners of a frame** | Drag: resizes the window (23) | — | — |
| **Inside a window** | Select, click, drag | Pastes the text selected last | The program's context menu, if it has one |
| **A slot of the Front Panel** | Starts its program; the arrow above opens the drawer (22) | — | On the clock: analog or digital (22) |
| **A workspace button** | Switches to the workspace; on the current one: renames it (24) | — | Rename (24) |
| **A minimised window's icon** | Brings the window back (23) | — | — |

On a touchpad, the right button is a click at the bottom right or with two fingers; the middle button is often a click with three fingers, or both buttons at once (chapter 21). Left-handed users swap the buttons on the *Mouse* page of Settings (chapter 33).

## Locked screen and clipboard history

| Keys | Locked screen (26) | Clipboard history (36) |
|---|---|---|
| ++enter++ | Unlocks with the password typed | Puts the chosen entry on the clipboard |
| ++backspace++ | Deletes the last character | — |
| ++esc++ | Clears what was typed | Closes the window |
| ++1++ to ++9++ | — | Chooses the first nine entries |
| ++delete++ | — | Removes the chosen entry |

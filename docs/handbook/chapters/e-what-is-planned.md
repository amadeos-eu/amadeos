# E. What Is Planned

The *Planned* boxes of this book, collected in one place, sorted by where they belong. The milestones (M12 and so on) are those of the [ROADMAP](https://github.com/datazuul/amadeos/blob/main/ROADMAP.md), where the plan is kept up to date. Contributions to any of them are welcome.

## The applications

| Program | What comes next | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| File Manager | Folders that refresh by themselves, quick search, tabs and bookmarks, an icon view with a folder tree, drag and drop, *Open With…*, archives as folders, folders on other computers | 41 |
| WritePad | Opening and saving OpenDocument, Word and RTF; pictures, header and footer, print preview, *Find*, tables, spell checking, PDF | 4, 43 |
| Mailer | New mail shown at once, folder management, drafts and templates, search, threads, printing, reading without a network | 44 |
| Calendar | One place for all accounts, other calendars next to your own, keeping in step with a CalDAV server | 45 |
| Contacts | Addresses completed in the Mailer, attendees and birthdays in the Calendar, keeping in step with a CardDAV server, merging two contacts by hand | 45 |
| Video Player | A face like the Audio Station's, DVD titles and chapters, playlists, audio and subtitle tracks, full screen, volume | 46 |
| Image Viewer | A picture as the backdrop of a workspace, moving GIF and WebP pictures, HEIC photos, cropping, brightness and contrast, editing title and keywords | 47 |
| Image Scan | Several pages into one PDF, brightness and contrast, straightening, the scanner's button | 47 |
| PDF Viewer | A night mode, filling in forms, highlighting and notes, comic archives inside PDF | 48 |
| Books | A scrolling view, comics in CBR and PDF form, notes and highlighting, tables and vertical writing | 48 |
| Calculator | A mode for converting units, later currencies | 48 |
| Documentation Viewer | A table of contents of all documentation, full-text search, manual pages, bookmarks | 48 |

!!! tip
    What is planned changes as amadeOS grows. The chapter of each item has the details; the ROADMAP has the current state.

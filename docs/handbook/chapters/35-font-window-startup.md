# 35. Font, Window and Startup

CDE's Style Manager had three more controls. amadeOS shows their buttons, greyed out, so that the Style Manager looks familiar and you know what is coming.

!!! planned "Planned: Font"
    Larger or smaller text for the whole desktop and the amadeOS applications, in a few steps, as CDE's *Font* control offered. Until then, Firefox and this handbook zoom with ++ctrl+plus++ and ++ctrl+minus++.

!!! planned "Planned: Window"
    How a window gets the keyboard focus (by a click or by moving the mouse over it), whether a window comes to the front when you click into it, and where minimized windows go.

!!! planned "Planned: Startup"
    What the desktop does when you log in and out: whether it opens the windows of your last session again, or always starts with an empty desktop. Until then, programs that should start at every login can be added to the autostart (chapter 55).

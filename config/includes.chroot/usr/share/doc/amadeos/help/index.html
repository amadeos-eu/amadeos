amadeOS Desktop Help

The amadeOS desktop follows the Common Desktop Environment (CDE): a front panel at the bottom of the screen, four workspaces and framed windows in the Motif style.

The front panel

Click a slot to start its main application. Slots with a small triangle (▲) above them have a drawer: click the triangle to open it and choose one of its entries.

Clock and calendarThe current time and date. Click the calendar for today's date.
File Manageramade-files in your home folder. The drawer opens your home folder or the root of the file system.
Text Editoramade-pad and the other installed text editors.
Mail & Internetamade-mail. The drawer also holds the other installed mail programs and the Internet applications, like the web browser Firefox ESR.
Sound & Video, GraphicsThe installed multimedia and graphics applications.
Workspace switcherThe four workspaces, the screen lock, the language of the desktop and EXIT to log out, shut down or restart the computer.
PrinterPrinters and print jobs.
ToolsThe Application Manager (all installed applications by category). The drawer also holds the Style Manager (colors, backdrop, fonts, mouse, keyboard, screen saver), a terminal, a root console, the session logs and the system information.
HelpThis help and the other installed documentation.
Trash CanThe deleted files, shown in amade-files.
StatusThe network connection. Click it to see and choose networks.

Workspaces

Each of the four workspaces has its own windows. Switch between them with the buttons in the middle of the front panel.

Windows

Title barDrag it to move the window. The buttons on its right minimize, maximize and close the window; the button on its left opens the window menu.
BorderDrag a border or corner to resize the window.
Minimized windowsappear as icons on the desktop. Click one to restore its window.

The desktop menu

Right-click the desktop background for the workspace menu: start a terminal, the text editor or the file manager, shuffle, minimize or restore the windows, open the Style Manager, refresh the desktop, lock the screen or log out.

Keyboard

Super+LLock the screen.

Logging in and out

At the login screen, choose your language before logging in. EXIT in the front panel logs out, drops to a text console, shuts down or restarts the computer.
